Questions & Details

FAQGuest Guide

everything you need to know for the day
What time should we arrive?
We recommend arriving by 4:00 PM so you have enough time to settle in before the nuptial ceremony begins at 4:30 PM.
Are the nuptials and reception at the same venue?
Yes! Both the nuptial ceremony and reception will take place at Gloria Banquets, Ghodbunder Road, Thane. You won't need to travel between venues.
Is parking available at Gloria Banquets?
Yes! Gloria Banquets provides dedicated parking facilities and on-site valet assistance for arriving guests.
What is the nearest railway station or transit point?
Thane Railway Station is the nearest transit hub, approximately 20–25 minutes away by taxi or auto via Ghodbunder Road.
Are children welcome?
Absolutely! We would love to celebrate with your entire family. Please ensure you update the guest count in the RSVP form so we can arrange adequate seating.
Can we take photos and share them?
Yes! We would be delighted to see the celebration through your eyes. Feel free to take photos and share them using our official hashtags: #fernsandpetals and #fnpincovenant.
Is there a dress code for the wedding?
We encourage formal evening wear or elegant festive attire. Feel free to dress comfortably while keeping the occasion in mind.
Whom can we contact if we need assistance on the wedding day?
If you need help with directions, parking or any other arrangements, please get in touch with the family or the person who shared the invitation with you.
